## 浅色 · 通用设置

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one extremely high-fidelity desktop application settings UI concept image for the Chinese embedded development IDE "Nodu · 见微". It should look like a carefully finished, shippable application screenshot, not a wireframe, not a promotional poster. Visual direction: very close to the restrained DeepSeek Harness desktop settings design: native system sans-serif typography resembling SF Pro and PingFang SC, regular-weight 14px body / 22px line-height, medium-weight 16px /24px headings, 12px /18px secondary labels, natural Chinese letter spacing. Clean white or chip-ink surfaces, quiet neutral fills, exceptionally precise alignment, generous but purposeful whitespace, thin line icons, soft rounded controls, no gradients. Retain Nodu identity with cobalt blue #2F5BFF used sparingly for switches and focus.
Composition: landscape 1536x1024, a straight-on pixel-sharp digital UI screenshot. One large centered settings modal, approximately 1000x820 image pixels, comfortably inset from edges. Rounded 24px panel corners, subtle realistic soft drop shadow. A dimmed very low-contrast Nodu IDE is visible around it, with faint file tree, C editor and AI chat sidebar; keep the background subordinate. No perspective, device mockup, desktop photograph, cursor, annotations, callout arrows, watermarks or invented marketing copy.
Modal structure: left navigation about 23% width, title "设置" at top left, only two navigation items "通用" with a thin gear icon and "AI" with a thin sparkle icon. Soft neutral rounded selected state. No vertical dividing border. Small close X at top right. Right content column aligned to a shared 24px inset. Beautiful optical alignment, balanced vertical rhythm, concise exact Chinese labels. Do not make everything bold. Thin 16px outline icons. Consistent 36px form controls, 28px icon buttons, 36x20 switches, 8-12px control corners. All text must be crisp and readable, professionally typeset.
Theme: light, pure white modal #FFFFFF, subdued cool gray #F7F8FA and #F0F2F5 controls, dark text #1D2129, secondary #5B616C, faint border #E0E3E8.
Selected navigation: "通用". Right content title "通用". Six thoughtfully spaced settings rows:

"模式" segmented selector "极简" and selected "专业".
A very light separator after these two rows.
"字号" with compact minus button, centered "14", plus button.
"自动保存" with a blue ON switch on the right.
"自动换行" with a blue ON switch on the right.
"缩进" with segmented values "2", selected "4", "8".
Bottom of this group: quiet separator and clickable row "全部设置" with right chevron. No save/cancel footer, settings apply immediately. Keep each row neatly aligned with 56-64px logical row height. Compact selected segments, calm and polished. Leave some white breathing room below, but avoid a giant empty lower half.
